About

I'm a full-stack developer based in France, working mainly with the MERN stack. I started with a front-end development program at Studi in February 2025 and graduated in April 2026. I then moved to the back end because I wanted to build complete products, not just interfaces.

Today I build React front ends, Node.js and Express APIs, and MongoDB databases, and I enjoy connecting all three into something that really works. My projects, including a restaurant app and a hotel booking app, are on GitHub, and most of them are deployed online.

I'm currently open to full-time positions and freelance projects. I enjoy collaborating with a team, I can also work independently, and I'm always eager to learn new tools.
